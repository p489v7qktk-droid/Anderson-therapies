My work brings together communication, relationships and wellbeing, starting with what matters to you.

Online cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT)

I work with children, young people and adults experiencing anxiety, low mood, obsessive compulsive disorder (OCD) and phobias.

CBT helps us explore how thoughts, feelings and actions connect, and try practical ways of responding to difficulties. We agree what you would like help with and shape the work together.

My particular interest is making therapy accessible by adapting it to your communication preferences. Drawing on my background in speech and language therapy, this might include clearer language, visual supports, written information, more processing time or different ways of expressing yourself.

You do not need a diagnosis or an identified communication difficulty to work with me.

Sessions take place online. For children and young people, we discuss whether online sessions are suitable and how parents or carers might be involved.

£90 per 50-minute session.

Communication and wellbeing assessments

Sometimes everyday conversations, relationships or situations feel difficult, and it is not clear why.

I work mainly with children and young people, alongside the people important to them, to understand communication strengths, preferences and support needs. Adult enquiries are also welcome so we can discuss whether my experience fits what is needed.

Depending on what matters most, we may explore speech and language, thoughts and feelings, sensory experiences, relationships and the environments where communication happens.

Together, we build a shared understanding of what is happening and identify practical changes. This may include a communication profile, recommendations for more accessible support, or ideas for families, carers, schools and other professionals to try.

Face-to-face assessments and commissioned work are available by arrangement, with location agreed before booking.

What might bring you here?

You might want help understanding:

  • Why conversations keep ending in frustration.
  • Whether communication preferences are being missed or misunderstood.
  • How therapy, education, work or community spaces could be made more accessible.

These are questions we can explore together.

Need urgent help?

Anderson Therapies is not an emergency or crisis service. Messages are not monitored continuously. Please do not use the enquiry form or wait for a reply if you need urgent help.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E.

For urgent mental health support in England, call NHS 111 and select the mental health option, or contact your GP for an urgent appointment.
