You said:

contact journey and social-sharing information. this was on my to do list re the webiste updates ... any ideas what this meant/

ChatGPT said:

I think it was two separate website checks:

  1. Contact journey
    Make it obvious how an organisation, partner or commissioner moves from reading about your work to contacting you.

    For your site, that likely meant:

    • Clear buttons such as “Discuss a project” or “Explore working together”

    • Buttons leading to your email/contact page

    • Clarifying what people should include in an enquiry

    • A short privacy line because enquiries are sent by email rather than through a form

  2. Social-sharing information
    Check what appears when someone shares your website on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Facebook, etc.:

    • Page title

    • Short description

    • Preview image/logo

    • Correct website link

This is usually called social preview or Open Graph information. It’s separate from adding the LinkedIn icon to your footer, which we discussed today.

I’d rewrite the to-do item more clearly as:

Check the contact journey and set the website’s social-sharing preview image, title and description.

So the LinkedIn footer link partly addresses the social side, but you should still test what your homepage looks like when its URL is pasted into LinkedIn or WhatsApp.
