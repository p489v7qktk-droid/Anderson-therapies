# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately to
sabrinaanderson@andersontherapies.co.uk. Include the affected page or file,
steps to reproduce the issue, and any relevant screenshots or logs. Do not
publish suspected vulnerabilities before they have been reviewed.

The general contact form does not accept credentials or payment details. The
Parent Space booking form collects booking information and optional
accessibility details through the Supabase booking service; it never collects
card details or payment credentials. Payments are made directly through Tide.
