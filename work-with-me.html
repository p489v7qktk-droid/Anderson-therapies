My Approach

Every project is different, but my approach is consistent. It has grown through clinical practice, consultancy, collaboration and continual learning. I believe that when people come together around shared values and what matters, they can build understanding, strengthen communication and create lasting change.

Bring People Together

Bringing people together creates space to explore what matters, hear different perspectives and develop shared understanding. I create opportunities for people to contribute, reflect and learn from one another. Through this process, ideas emerge, relationships strengthen and meaningful change becomes possible.

What Matters Shapes Communication

Communication matters in different ways at different times. It can take centre stage, hold relationships together, express identity or support a sense of belonging. At other times, rest, safety or simply being present may matter more. I begin by understanding what matters to the people involved and exploring the role communication might play. This makes communication shared, meaningful and relevant to their lives.

Create Together

The people closest to an experience often hold the greatest insight. I develop approaches alongside children, young people, families, practitioners, organisations and communities, drawing on their strengths, priorities, cultures and ways of communicating. The aim is to create something meaningful, sustainable and owned by the people involved.

Keep Learning

Every project offers opportunities to learn through curiosity, reflection, shared experience and listening. Whether learning happens within a local community or with international partners, it can deepen understanding, strengthen relationships and open new possibilities for communication, connection and belonging.
