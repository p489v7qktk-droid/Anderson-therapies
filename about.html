About Me

Founder: Sabrina Anderson

I’m a Speech and Language Therapist and Cognitive Behavioural Psychotherapist having worked across communication, mental health, education and community development.

My experience spans clinical practice, service design, leadership, consultancy and workforce development across health, education and community settings. The thread connecting this work is communication, connection and belonging.

I am interested in creating the conditions for inclusive communication to flourish, helping people build relationships, strengthen identity and experience belonging. This thinking has developed into the Communication for Connection Framework, an emerging approach shaped through practice, collaboration and shared learning.

One of the projects I am most proud of is co-producing a selective or situational mutism pathway and resource hub with children, young people, families and professionals. It demonstrated how lived experience and professional knowledge can come together to shape meaningful communication pathways.

Over a decade after qualifying as a speech and language therapist, I continue to bring different perspectives together, support shared learning and develop approaches that are sustainable and owned by the people involved.
