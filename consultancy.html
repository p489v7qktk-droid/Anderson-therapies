Consultancy

Every organisation is different.

Some are looking for a fresh perspective. Others are developing new services, strengthening existing practice or creating opportunities for people to learn together.

Anderson Therapies works alongside organisations at local, regional, national and international levels to strengthen communication, connection and belonging.

Every project is collaborative, shaped by curiosity, shared learning and a commitment to creating meaningful change.

Areas of Work

Projects may include:

  • Communication, connection and belonging
  • Communication and mental health
  • Inclusive communication and participation
  • Workforce development and bespoke training
  • Service design and development
  • Co-production and collaborative practice
  • Leadership development and systems thinking
  • Communities of practice and shared learning
  • Bespoke projects developed in partnership with organisations

Ways of Working

Support is tailored to the needs of each organisation and may include:

  • Consultancy to develop communication-informed services, pathways and systems of support
  • Bespoke training that strengthens knowledge, confidence and practical skills
  • Clinical consultation to support practitioners working at the intersection of communication and mental health
  • Supervision, coaching and reflective practice for professionals working with communication, connection, belonging and participation
  • Facilitation of collaborative learning, co-production and communities of practice
  • Project design, evaluation and service improvement
  • Speaking engagements
  • Collaboration on funded or commissioned projects

No two projects are the same. The starting point is always to understand what matters, explore the context and work together to develop an approach that fits.

Examples of Work

Training and published resources

  • Co-authored Mind Your Words, an eLearning course hosted by the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists, for speech and language therapists and other professionals supporting children and young people with communication and mental health needs.
  • Co-delivered a session at an annual local council conference for early years practitioners exploring practitioner wellbeing through the Solihull Approach.
  • Delivered training for National Health Service mental health staff on supporting children and young people with selective or situational mutism.
  • Designed and delivered training for local council and National Health Service therapists and practitioners in children’s mental health services on the relationship between communication and mental health.
  • Co-authored the Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists’ Supporting children and young people’s mental health services factsheet.

Service and pathway development

  • Co-produced a multi-agency selective or situational mutism pathway with children, young people, families and professionals.
  • Led a service-development project exploring the role of speech and language therapy across children and young people’s mental health pathways, using consultation, surveys and stakeholder engagement (including children and parents) to inform recommendations. This work was written up in The role of SLTs in child and adolescent mental health services, published in the RCSLT Bulletin.
  • Developing a parent space where parents can explore practical, creative and culturally meaningful ways to support their children’s communication and mental health.

Coaching, supervision and reflective practice

  • Provided one-to-one coaching for National Health Service child and adolescent mental health therapists and practitioners on making psychological therapy and mental health support more communication accessible.
  • Provided one-to-one supervision for specialists working in children and young people’s mental health services.
  • Led reflective-practice group sessions for speech and language therapists working in local council youth justice services.

National leadership

  • Supporting national collaboration, workforce development and shared learning as a Steering Group member of the Children & Young People Allied Health Professionals Clinical Leadership Network.
