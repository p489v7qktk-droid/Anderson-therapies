The Parent Space

South Hertfordshire

A small, welcoming space for parents of children with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) to pause, connect and explore practical responses to anxiety, overwhelm and emotional distress.

A different kind of parent space

The Parent Space will combine conversation and reflection with music, drumming, movement and other creative activities. It will draw on communication, sensory processing and parent-led cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), offering an experience that is practical, creative and grounded.

The introductory event will be for adults only. No experience of drumming or movement will be needed, and activities will be adaptable.
