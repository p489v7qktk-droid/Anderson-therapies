Privacy Policy

Anderson Therapies Ltd is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly, transparently and in accordance with UK data protection law.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Anderson Therapies Ltd may collect, why it is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

Anderson Therapies Ltd aims to collect only the personal information necessary to provide services, respond to enquiries and meet legal and professional responsibilities.

Anderson Therapies Ltd

Anderson Therapies Ltd provides consultancy, training, workforce development, service design, collaborative projects and freelance clinical services focused on communication, inclusion, mental health and participation.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how personal information is handled, please contact:

Email: sabrinaanderson@andersontherapies.co.uk

Information that may be collected

Anderson Therapies Ltd may collect the following information when you make contact or use Anderson Therapies' services:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organisation
  • Job title (where relevant)
  • Telephone number (if provided)
  • Any information you choose to include in your enquiry or correspondence

The website does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

How personal information is used

Personal information may be used to:

  • respond to enquiries
  • discuss potential consultancy, training, collaboration or commissioned work
  • deliver consultancy, training and other professional services
  • deliver contracted clinical services on behalf of other organisations
  • maintain appropriate business and professional records
  • develop and improve services, resources and professional practice
  • reflect on professional work to inform the ongoing development of the Communication for Connection Framework and related approaches
  • develop educational resources, conference presentations, publications and other professional outputs
  • comply with legal, regulatory and professional obligations, including safeguarding responsibilities where applicable

Learning, publications and professional development

Continuous learning is central to Anderson Therapies Ltd. Insights gained through consultancy, training and collaborative work may contribute to the ongoing development of the Communication for Connection Framework, professional resources, conference presentations, publications and other educational materials.

Where learning from practice is shared publicly, it will normally be presented in an anonymised form so that individuals cannot be identified. If Anderson Therapies Ltd wishes to use identifiable information, quotations or case studies, appropriate permission or consent will be obtained where required.

Clinical services delivered on behalf of other organisations

Anderson Therapies Ltd may provide freelance clinical services under contract to healthcare providers, educational settings and other organisations. Where services are delivered on behalf of another organisation, personal information is managed using that organisation's approved clinical systems, policies and procedures. Clinical records, reports and correspondence are maintained within the commissioning organisation's secure systems. Where applicable, the commissioning organisation's Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, processed, stored and retained.

Legal basis for processing

Anderson Therapies Ltd processes personal information where there is a lawful basis to do so. This may include:

  • Legitimate interests
  • Performance of a contract
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Consent (where required)

Sharing personal information

Anderson Therapies Ltd does not sell or share personal information for marketing purposes.

Personal information will only be shared where necessary to deliver services, where required by law, where necessary to protect an individual or meet safeguarding responsibilities, or with trusted service providers or commissioning organisations where this is necessary to deliver contracted services.

Keeping information secure

Anderson Therapies Ltd takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

This includes, where appropriate:

  • using password-protected devices
  • using multi-factor authentication where available
  • keeping software and devices up to date
  • using secure systems provided by commissioning organisations when delivering contracted clinical services
  • keeping business devices secure and protected against unauthorised access

Professional standards

Anderson Therapies Ltd is committed to maintaining high standards of confidentiality, information governance and professional practice. Services are delivered in accordance with relevant legal, ethical and professional responsibilities, including those relating to confidentiality, safeguarding and data protection.

How long information is kept

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, meet legal or regulatory requirements, or resolve any queries relating to the services provided.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • request access to your personal information
  • request correction of inaccurate information
  • request deletion of personal information where appropriate
  • object to certain types of processing
  • request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in legislation or the services provided by Anderson Therapies Ltd. The most recent version will always be published on this website.
