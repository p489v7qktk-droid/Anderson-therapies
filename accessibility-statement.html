Accessibility Statement

Accessibility Statement

Anderson Therapies Ltd is committed to making this website as accessible, inclusive and easy to use as possible.

Communication and accessibility are at the heart of Anderson Therapies Ltd. This website will continue to evolve with those principles in mind, with ongoing improvements made to support accessibility and inclusion.

Accessibility aims

Anderson Therapies Ltd aims to:

  • use clear, straightforward language
  • organise information using logical headings and page structure
  • maintain good colour contrast to support readability
  • ensure the website works across a range of devices
  • provide a consistent and easy-to-navigate experience
  • continually review and improve the accessibility of the website

Alternative formats

If you are unable to access information on this website or would like information in an alternative format, please get in touch.

Anderson Therapies Ltd will make reasonable efforts to provide information in a format that meets your needs.

Feedback

Feedback is welcomed and helps improve the accessibility of this website.

If you experience any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact:

Anderson Therapies Ltd

Email: info@andersontherapies.co.uk

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. As Anderson Therapies Ltd develops new content, resources and services, accessibility will continue to be considered and improvements made where possible.
